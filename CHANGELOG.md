# Changelog

## Schnapsen 0.5.0 – Public Preview

The first public test version of Schnapsen, a native GNOME card game by the Linux DS Team.

### Features

- Austrian two-player Schnapsen against the computer
- Six rule profiles and three computer difficulty levels
- Double German and French card faces with seven independently selectable card backs
- Automatic saving and resuming
- German and English interface
- Native GNOME interface with mouse, keyboard, and touch support

### Known limitations

- Court cards use letters and suit symbols while traditional illustrations are still pending.
- Sound effects and animations are planned for future versions.
- Further gameplay testing is ongoing.

Feedback and bug reports are welcome. [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/linux-ds/schnapsen/issues)
